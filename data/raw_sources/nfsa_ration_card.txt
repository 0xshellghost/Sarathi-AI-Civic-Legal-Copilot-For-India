NATIONAL FOOD SECURITY ACT 2013 & RATION CARD / PDS GRIEVANCE REDRESSAL
Authority: Department of Food and Public Distribution, Government of India
URL: https://nfsa.gov.in | Tier: 1 | Jurisdiction: Central / All India

1. Food Entitlements (Section 3 of NFSA 2013):
- Priority Household (PHH) Ration Card holders: 5 kg foodgrains (rice/wheat/coarse grains) per person per month.
- Antyodaya Anna Yojana (AAY) poorest of poor households: 35 kg foodgrains per household per month.
- Under Pradhan Mantri Garib Kalyan Anna Yojana (PMGKAY), foodgrains under NFSA are provided FREE of cost across all fair price shops (FPS).

2. One Nation One Ration Card (ONORC):
Enables migrant workers and beneficiaries to lift their entitled foodgrains from ANY fair price shop (FPS) in India using biometric (Aadhaar) authentication.

3. Redressal of Delayed/Denied Ration Card:
- District Grievance Redressal Officer (DGRO): Every district has an appointed DGRO under Section 15 of NFSA to hear complaints of non-issuance, wrongful deletion, or refusal of foodgrains.
- State Food Commission: Statutory appellate authority for NFSA grievances.
- National Toll-Free Food Helpline: 1967 or 1800-series state helpline.
- Anamodit (Pending) Applications: Food & Civil Supplies Department has a standard citizen charter of 30 days to process, verify, and approve/reject ration card applications. If delayed, citizen can file grievance on State PDS portal, CPGRAMS, or file an RTI under Section 6(1) seeking daily progress report.
