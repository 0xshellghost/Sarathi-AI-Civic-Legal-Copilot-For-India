PRADHAN MANTRI MATRU VANDANA YOJANA (PMMVY) - MATERNITY BENEFIT SCHEME
Authority: Ministry of Women and Child Development, Government of India
URL: https://pmmvy.wcd.gov.in | Tier: 1 | Jurisdiction: Central / All India

1. Benefit Structure:
- First Child: Cash incentive of ₹5,000 in two installments (₹3,000 on early registration + at least one ANC; ₹2,000 on child birth registration + first cycle of immunization).
- Second Child (Girl Child): One-time cash incentive of ₹6,000 on birth of a girl child to encourage positive attitude towards girl children.
- Direct Benefit Transfer (DBT) to mother's Aadhaar-linked bank account.

2. Eligibility Criteria:
- Pregnant Women and Lactating Mothers (PW&LM) aged 19 years or older.
- Must belong to any of the following socially/economically disadvantaged categories:
  (i) Women having net family income less than ₹8,00,000 per annum (EWS / Income Certificate);
  (ii) Women holding MGNREGA Job Card;
  (iii) Women holding Kisan Samman Nidhi beneficiary card;
  (iv) Women holding e-Shram card;
  (v) Women holding PM-JAY Ayushman Bharat card;
  (vi) Women holding BPL / Antyodaya / Priority Ration Card;
  (vii) Women with benchmark disability (40%+);
  (viii) SC/ST women.

3. Exclusions:
Women in regular employment with Central Government, State Government, PSUs, or receiving similar maternity benefits under any other statutory law.

4. Application Process:
Apply online at https://pmmvy.wcd.gov.in or offline through nearest Anganwadi Centre (AWC) / ASHA worker with Mother-Child Protection (MCP) Card, Aadhaar, and Bank Passbook.
