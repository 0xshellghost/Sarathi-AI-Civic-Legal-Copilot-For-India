SAMADHAN & LABOUR GRIEVANCE REDRESSAL MECHANISMS
Authority: Chief Labour Commissioner (Central) & State Labour Departments
URL: https://samadhan.labour.gov.in | Tier: 1 | Jurisdiction: Central / All India

1. What is the SAMADHAN Portal?
SAMADHAN (Software Application for Monitoring and Disposal, Handling of Industrial Disputes) is the official online dispute resolution platform under the Ministry of Labour and Employment.

2. How to File an Industrial Dispute or Wage Grievance:
- File online via https://samadhan.labour.gov.in or approach the local District Labour Officer / Conciliation Officer.
- Conciliation proceedings are scheduled within 14 to 30 days.
- Shram Suvidha Portal (https://shramsuvidha.gov.in) provides unified registration and inspection reporting.

3. National Helpline for Workers:
Ministry of Labour control rooms & toll-free numbers help stranded, unpaid, and migrant workers across India.
