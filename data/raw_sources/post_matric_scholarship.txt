POST-MATRIC SCHOLARSHIP FOR SC / OBC STUDENTS (NATIONAL SCHOLARSHIP PORTAL)
Authority: Ministry of Social Justice and Empowerment / Ministry of Tribal Affairs
URL: https://scholarships.gov.in | Tier: 1 | Jurisdiction: Central / All India

1. Purpose & Financial Coverage:
Provides full tuition fee reimbursement and monthly maintenance allowance for Scheduled Caste (SC), Scheduled Tribe (ST), and Other Backward Class (OBC/EBC) students pursuing post-matriculation or post-secondary courses in recognized colleges/universities.

2. Eligibility Criteria:
- Student must belong to SC, ST, or OBC/EBC category with valid caste certificate.
- Must have passed Class 10 (Matriculation) or higher qualifying examination.
- Total family annual income from all sources must not exceed ₹2,50,000 per annum (Income Certificate issued by competent authority required).
- Student must be studying in a recognized government or private educational institution.

3. Documents Required:
(i) Student Aadhaar Card (linked to bank account with DBT active);
(ii) Caste Certificate issued by Sub-Divisional Magistrate (SDM) / Tehsildar;
(iii) Income Certificate for the current financial year;
(iv) Marksheet of previous qualifying exam;
(v) College Fee Receipt & Bonafide Student Certificate;
(vi) Bank Account Passbook.

4. Application Mode:
Apply exclusively through National Scholarship Portal (NSP: https://scholarships.gov.in) with Institute verification.
