AADHAAR (UIDAI) UPDATE, CORRECTION AND CITIZEN GRIEVANCE GUIDELINES
Authority: Unique Identification Authority of India (UIDAI), Government of India
URL: https://uidai.gov.in | Tier: 1 | Jurisdiction: Central / All India

1. Online vs Offline Updates:
- Online via myAadhaar Portal (https://myaadhaar.uidai.gov.in): Address update, document update (Proof of Identity & Proof of Address), language change. Free document updates periodically announced.
- Aadhaar Seva Kendra / Enrolment Centre (Biometric): Mobile number update, email update, name correction (limited to 2 times), date of birth correction (limited to 1 time), gender correction (limited to 1 time), photo & biometric updates (mandatory at age 5 and 15).

2. Timelines & Tracking:
- Standard processing time: 90% of updates completed within 30 days.
- Tracking using 14-digit Enrolment ID (EID) or Update Request Number (URN).

3. Grievance Redressal:
- UIDAI Toll-Free Helpline: 1947 (24x7 IVRS, agent support 7am-11pm).
- Online Grievance: https://myaadhaar.uidai.gov.in/check-complaint-status
- Email: help@uidai.gov.in
