CODE ON WAGES, 2019 & PAYMENT OF WAGES RULES
Authority: Ministry of Labour and Employment, Government of India
URL: https://labour.gov.in | Tier: 1 | Jurisdiction: Central / All India

1. Timely Payment of Wages (Section 17):
- Daily-rated workers: Wages must be paid at the end of the shift.
- Weekly-rated workers: Wages must be paid on the last working day of the week.
- Monthly-rated employees: Wages must be paid before the expiry of the 7th or 10th day of the succeeding month depending on establishment size.

2. Prohibition of Unauthorized Deductions (Section 18):
Deductions from wages are strictly limited to statutory deductions (PF, ESI, TDS), absence from duty, and actual damage/loss after giving employee a fair show-cause opportunity. Total deductions cannot exceed 50% of total wages in any wage period.

3. Final Settlement on Termination / Resignation (Section 17(2)):
Where an employee is removed, dismissed, retrenched, or resigns from service, the wages and dues must be paid within TWO working days of termination or resignation.

4. Redressal Mechanism:
Employees can file a claim before the Authority appointed under Section 45 (Labour Commissioner / Assistant Labour Commissioner). Authority can award unpaid wages plus compensation up to 10 times the amount of unpaid wages.
