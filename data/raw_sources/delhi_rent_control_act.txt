DELHI RENT CONTROL ACT, 1958 & DELHI TENANCY PRACTICES
Authority: Government of NCT of Delhi / Delhi High Court
URL: https://delhi.gov.in | Tier: 1 | Jurisdiction: Delhi

1. Tenant Security Deposit & Agreement:
Under Section 106 of Transfer of Property Act 1882 & Rent Control guidelines in Delhi, monthly residential tenancies are terminable by 15 days notice expiring with the end of a month of tenancy.
Security deposit (usually 1 to 2 months rent) is held in trust by the landlord and must be returned at the end of the tenancy after peaceful handover, minus legitimate electricity bills or actual documented damages.

2. Unlawful Lockout or Service Disconnection:
Landlords in Delhi are prohibited under Section 45 of Delhi Rent Control Act from cutting off water, electricity, or sanitary connections. An application under Section 45 before the Rent Controller can secure immediate restoration orders with police assistance.

3. Deposit Recovery Remedy:
If a landlord in Delhi refuses to return the security deposit after handover, tenant can issue a Legal Demand Notice giving 15 days time. If unheeded, tenant can file a Summary Suit for Recovery under Order 37 CPC in the District Court or approach Delhi Dispute Resolution Society (DDRS) / Consumer Court if premises were rented as serviced accommodation.
