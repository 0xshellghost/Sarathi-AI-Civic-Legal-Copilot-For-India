MAHARASHTRA RENT CONTROL ACT, 1999 (Maharashtra Act No. XVIII of 2000)
Authority: Government of Maharashtra
URL: https://housing.maharashtra.gov.in | Tier: 1 | Jurisdiction: Maharashtra

1. Security Deposit & Leave and License:
- Under Section 55, all Leave and License agreements must be in writing and registered compulsorily before the Sub-Registrar. Responsibility of registration lies on the landlord/licensor.
- Security deposit refund: Must be refunded upon expiry/termination of license and handover of premises. Landlord withholding deposit without valid legal grounds gives tenant right to initiate recovery before the Competent Authority or Civil Court with interest.

2. Prohibition Against Cutting Essential Supplies (Section 29):
No landlord, either himself or through any person, shall without just or sufficient cause cut off or withhold any essential supply or service enjoyed by the tenant.
If breached, tenant can file complaint before the Court/Rent Controller. Penalty includes imprisonment up to 3 months or fine up to ₹1,000 or both.

3. Eviction Protections (Section 15 & 16):
Landlord cannot recover possession as long as tenant pays or is ready and willing to pay standard rent and permitted increases. Eviction notice under Transfer of Property Act / Rent Control Act requires 15-30 days formal notice and adjudication before Court of Small Causes / Rent Controller.
