PRADHAN MANTRI KISAN SAMMAN NIDHI (PM-KISAN) SCHEME
Authority: Ministry of Agriculture and Farmers Welfare, Government of India
URL: https://pmkisan.gov.in | Tier: 1 | Jurisdiction: Central / All India

1. Scheme Objective & Financial Benefit:
PM-KISAN provides income support of ₹6,000 per year to all landholding farmer families across India, payable in three equal four-monthly installments of ₹2,000 each directly transferred into Aadhaar-seeded bank accounts (DBT).

2. Eligibility Criteria:
- All landholding farmer families who own cultivable land registered in their name in the State/UT land records.
- Aadhaar card mandatory.
- Land ownership records (Khatauni / Jamabandi / 7/12 extract) verified by state revenue officials.
- Active bank account linked with Aadhaar and NPCI DBT enabled.

3. Exclusion Criteria (Higher Economic Status):
(i) All institutional landholders;
(ii) Farmer families where one or more members hold or held constitutional posts;
(iii) Former and present Ministers, MPs, MLAs, MLCs, Mayors, Chairpersons of District Panchayats;
(iv) Serving or retired officers and employees of Central/State Government, Ministries, PSUs, Autonomous bodies (excluding Class IV / Multi Tasking Staff);
(v) All superannuated/retired pensioners whose monthly pension is ₹10,000 or more;
(vi) All persons who paid Income Tax in the last assessment year;
(vii) Professionals like Doctors, Engineers, Lawyers, Chartered Accountants, and Architects registered with professional bodies.

4. Application Process:
- Self-registration on PM-KISAN Portal (https://pmkisan.gov.in) under 'Farmers Corner' -> 'New Farmer Registration'.
- Can also apply via local Common Service Centres (CSCs) or State Agriculture Officer.
- Documents required: Aadhaar Card, Land Record Copy, Bank Passbook, Mobile Number.
- Mandatory e-KYC via OTP on portal or biometric at CSC.
