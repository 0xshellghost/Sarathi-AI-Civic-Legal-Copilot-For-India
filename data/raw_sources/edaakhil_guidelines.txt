E-DAAKHIL CONSUMER DISPUTES ONLINE FILING SYSTEM
Authority: National Consumer Disputes Redressal Commission (NCDRC) / NIC
URL: https://edaakhil.nic.in | Tier: 1 | Jurisdiction: Central / All India

1. Digital Consumer Court Filing:
e-Daakhil allows consumers to file complaints, pay court fees online, upload digital evidence, track hearing notices, and attend virtual hearings before District, State, and National Consumer Commissions from home.

2. Mandatory Documents for Filing:
(i) Complaint petition detailing facts, deficiency in service/defect in goods, and compensation claim;
(ii) Verification affidavit signed by complainant;
(iii) Copy of purchase invoice, receipt, warranty card, or booking receipt;
(iv) Proof of communication with opposite party (emails, legal notice, NCH docket);
(v) Identity & Address proof of complainant (Aadhaar, Voter ID, Passport).

3. Statutory Fee Scale:
- Claims up to ₹5 Lakh: NIL (₹0 court fee).
- Claims ₹5 Lakh to ₹10 Lakh: ₹200.
- Claims ₹10 Lakh to ₹20 Lakh: ₹400.
- Claims ₹20 Lakh to ₹50 Lakh: ₹1,000.
- Payment accepted via Bharatkosh payment gateway (UPI, Net Banking, Card).
