EMPLOYEES PROVIDENT FUND (EPFO) WITHDRAWAL & EPFiGMS GRIEVANCES
Authority: Employees' Provident Fund Organisation (EPFO), Ministry of Labour and Employment
URL: https://epfigms.gov.in | Tier: 1 | Jurisdiction: Central / All India

1. Types of Claims & Online Settlement (Unified Member Portal):
- Form 19 (PF Final Settlement after leaving service / unemployment > 2 months).
- Form 10C (PFS Pension withdrawal benefit).
- Form 31 (PF Partial Advance for house purchase, medical emergency, marriage, education, natural calamity).
- Mandatory requirements: Active Universal Account Number (UAN), Aadhaar verified & seeded, bank account with IFSC seeded, mobile number linked.

2. Settlement Timelines:
- Statutory standard settlement timeline is 20 working days. Most auto-settled online claims process in 3 to 7 working days.

3. EPFiGMS (EPF Internet Grievance Management System):
- Official grievance portal: https://epfigms.gov.in
- Register grievance using UAN or PPO Number.
- Maximum redressal timeframe: 30 days.
- If dissatisfied with Regional PF Commissioner's response, citizen can file First Appeal on EPFiGMS or escalate to CPGRAMS / file RTI with Ministry of Labour.
