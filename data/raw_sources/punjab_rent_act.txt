PUNJAB RENT ACT, 1995 (Punjab Act No. 13 of 2012)
Authority: Government of Punjab / Department of Housing & Urban Development
URL: https://punjab.gov.in | Tier: 1 | Jurisdiction: Punjab

1. Tenancy Agreement & Registration (Section 4):
All tenancy agreements in Punjab must be executed in writing and registered with the Rent Authority of the respective subdivision/district within 30 days of execution.

2. Security Deposit Refund (Section 7):
Security deposit received by the landlord must not exceed one month's rent in residential tenancies. Landlord must refund the security deposit within one month of receiving vacant possession from the tenant. Unjustified withholding attracts interest at 15% per annum.

3. Essential Services Protection (Section 19):
Landlords cannot cut off essential amenities (water, electricity, access) without a lawful order from the Rent Authority. Rent Authority can impose fines up to ₹5,000 and direct immediate restoration.
